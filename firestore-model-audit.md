# Firestore model audit

Target project: `gen-lang-client-0169317507`

Target database: `ai-studio-gentedigital-cb816dee-4739-4dd8-8612-2cfe4702cf93`

Edition: Enterprise / Firestore Native (confirmed with `firestore:databases:list`).

## Current paths

- `members/{memberId}`: member summary used by the ranking and team views.
- `evaluations/{evaluationId}`: evaluation snapshot written by the callable
  `saveEvaluation`.
- `auditLogs/{auditId}`: append-only evaluation audit record with actor, cycle, score and revision.

## Current queries

- `members`, ordered by `score` descending, subscribed with `onSnapshot`; the
  client filters `deleted == true` after parsing so legacy documents without
  the field remain visible.
- Archived members use a separate `where('deleted', '==', true)` query.

## Required hardening

- No public reads or anonymous writes.
- Roles must come from a trusted custom Auth claim; clients must not self-assign roles.
- Member PII must not be exposed to unauthenticated users or shipped in the bundle.
- Evaluation IDs must be deterministic per member and cycle.
- Score and status must be validated on create and update.
- Client-side seed and silent local fallback must be removed.
- Timestamps should be generated by Firestore, not by the browser.
- Evaluation writes must advance the optimistic-concurrency `revision` exactly once.
- Audit records must be immutable and identify the authenticated actor.
- The client must not rely on a composite `deleted + score` index for the active
  members subscription.
